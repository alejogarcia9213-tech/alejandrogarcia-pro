Hinweis für neue Kopierer

Start with the right expectations.

Comienza con las expectativas correctas.

Beginnen Sie mit den richtigen Erwartungen.

Markets don't move in a straight line. If you start copying after a strong move, short-term volatility can happen. If your copy starts in red, that does not automatically mean the strategy is broken. The goal is to understand the process before judging a short period.

Los mercados no se mueven en línea recta. Si comienzas a copiar después de un movimiento fuerte, puede ocurrir volatilidad a corto plazo. Si tu copia comienza en rojo, eso no significa automáticamente que la estrategia esté rota. El objetivo es entender el proceso antes de juzgar un período corto.

Märkte bewegen sich nicht in einer geraden Linie. Wenn Sie nach einer starken Bewegung mit dem Kopieren beginnen, kann kurzfristige Volatilität auftreten. Wenn Ihre Kopie im Minus startet, bedeutet das nicht automatisch, dass die Strategie kaputt ist. Das Ziel ist es, den Prozess zu verstehen, bevor man einen kurzen Zeitraum beurteilt.

What is CopyTrading?

CopyTrading allows your invested amount to follow another investor's portfolio automatically, proportionally to your copy amount and subject to eToro's CopyTrading mechanics. You can review the investor, holdings, risk, updates, and performance inside eToro.

Is copy trading like investing in an ETF?

It can feel similar in the sense that your money follows a portfolio you did not build yourself. The difference is that an ETF usually tracks a passive basket or index, while CopyTrading follows a real investor's active portfolio decisions.

Is this suitable for beginners?

It may fit beginners who understand risk, can tolerate volatility, and want a long-term approach. It is not for people expecting guaranteed or fast returns.

Why is monthly investing important?

Because long-term wealth usually comes from consistency. A single deposit can help you start, but regular monthly investing helps build the base that gives compounding more power over time.

What happens during red markets?

Red markets are reviewed calmly. The focus is on business quality, risk control, position sizing, and avoiding emotional decisions.

No. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Investing involves risk, including loss of capital.
